What is what? Quick guide
  • Image the model that draws each scene. You need at least one; it works the same in narrated video and in image mode.
  • Retouch the models that change a picture of yours from an instruction. Optional: without them pictures are still generated.
  • LoRA an optional style tweak added on top of an image model. The pipeline does not apply it yet.
  • Video models that animate an image with real motion (Wan or AnimateDiff). A project uses them from its animation block.
  • Music the models that compose and play the songs in music mode. It is a separate feature from video and images.
  • Voice the voice that narrates the video. Only needed in video mode.

To get started, one Image model and one voice (the ones marked “Starter”) are enough: with those you can already generate images and narrated videos.
