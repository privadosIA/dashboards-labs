Take it from a video or audio

Choose a song (an mp3 or wav) or a video, and its lyrics, language, tempo and key are read and a style is proposed. It is a proposal: nothing is written into the track until you press “Apply”.

  • The lyrics are a transcription: review them. A sung voice is read worse than a spoken one, above all with instruments.
  • The tempo and the key are estimates: they sometimes come out at half or double, or in the relative key.
  • Use only material you have the rights to.
